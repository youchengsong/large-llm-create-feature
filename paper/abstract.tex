\begin{abstract}
Automated feature engineering (AutoFE) is critical for improving the performance of machine learning models on tabular data. Recent approaches leverage large language models (LLMs) to generate features, but they rely solely on the LLM's internal knowledge and lack structured domain expertise, rigorous verification, and traceability. In this paper, we propose \textbf{KnowFeat}, a knowledge-guided feature engineering framework that integrates hierarchical domain knowledge into an LLM-based agent to generate high-quality, interpretable features. KnowFeat introduces three key innovations: (1) a \emph{five-level knowledge hierarchy} ranging from schema-only metadata to risk indicators, detection rules, expert opinions, and court document evidence; (2) a \emph{three-stage verification pipeline} (L1: execution, L2: statistical quality, L3: model effectiveness) that ensures only genuinely useful features are accepted; and (3) a \emph{SHAP-guided search strategy} with failure memory and forward selection to efficiently explore the feature space. We evaluate KnowFeat on seven public datasets and a real-world anti-money laundering (AML) dataset with 200K transactions. On public benchmarks, KnowFeat achieves the best AUC on 4 out of 7 datasets and the best average rank (1.4) among seven methods including OpenFE, AutoFeat, CAAFE, FeatLLM, and LLM-FE, while generating 3--10$\times$ fewer features than FeatLLM. On the AML dataset, 18 knowledge-guided features improve Recall by +2.1pp and F1 by +1.5pp over a strong 93-feature baseline, outperforming methods that add up to 1,031 features. Ablation studies confirm that domain knowledge is essential, and every generated feature is traceable to specific knowledge assets.
\end{abstract}
