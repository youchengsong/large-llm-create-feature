\begin{abstract}
Automated feature engineering (AutoFE) is critical for improving the performance of machine learning models on tabular data. Recent approaches leverage large language models (LLMs) to generate features, but they rely solely on the LLM's internal knowledge and lack structured domain expertise, rigorous verification, and traceability. In this paper, we propose \textbf{KnowFeat}, a knowledge-guided feature engineering framework that integrates hierarchical domain knowledge into an LLM-based agent to generate high-quality, interpretable features. KnowFeat introduces three key innovations: (1) a \emph{five-level knowledge hierarchy} ranging from schema-only metadata to risk indicators, detection rules, expert opinions, and court document evidence, enabling controllable knowledge injection; (2) a \emph{three-stage verification pipeline} (L1: execution, L2: statistical quality, L3: model effectiveness) that ensures only genuinely useful features are accepted; and (3) a \emph{SHAP-guided dimension budgeting} strategy with failure memory and forward selection to efficiently explore the feature space. We evaluate KnowFeat on seven public datasets spanning finance, healthcare, and marketing domains. KnowFeat achieves the best AUC on four out of seven datasets and consistently ranks among the top two, while generating significantly fewer features than alternatives (12--46 vs.\ 98--1,124 for FeatLLM). Ablation studies confirm that domain knowledge is essential: schema-only generation produces zero features on some datasets, while the full knowledge hierarchy consistently improves performance. Every generated feature is traceable to specific knowledge assets, providing the auditability required in regulated domains such as finance and healthcare.
\end{abstract}
