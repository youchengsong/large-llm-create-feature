\begin{abstract}
Automated feature engineering (AutoFE) is critical for improving the performance of machine learning models on tabular data. Recent LLM-based approaches can generate semantically meaningful features, yet they lack hierarchical structured domain knowledge, rigorous multi-stage verification, and auditable provenance. We propose \textbf{KnowFeat}, a knowledge-guided feature engineering framework that integrates hierarchical domain knowledge into an LLM-based agent to generate high-quality, interpretable features with full traceability. KnowFeat introduces three key innovations: (1) a \emph{five-level knowledge hierarchy} ranging from schema-only metadata to risk indicators, detection rules, expert opinions, and court document evidence, organized in a structured format to prevent hallucination; (2) a \emph{three-stage verification pipeline} (L1: code execution sandbox, L2: statistical quality filtering, L3: model effectiveness evaluation) that ensures only genuinely useful features are accepted; and (3) \emph{auditable provenance}---every generated feature is accompanied by a provenance card that traces its creation to specific knowledge assets, reasoning chains, and computation steps, enabling the auditability required in regulated domains such as finance and healthcare. We evaluate KnowFeat on seven public datasets and a real-world anti-money laundering (AML) dataset with 200K transactions. On public benchmarks, KnowFeat achieves the best AUC on 4 of 7 datasets and the best average rank (1.4) among seven methods. On the AML dataset, 18 knowledge-guided features improve Recall by +2.1pp and F1 by +1.5pp over a strong 93-feature baseline, outperforming methods that add up to 1,031 features. Ablation studies confirm that domain knowledge is essential for consistent feature generation.
\end{abstract}
