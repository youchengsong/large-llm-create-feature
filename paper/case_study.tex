\section{Case Study: Knowledge-Grounded Features}
\label{sec:case_study}

We present two representative case studies from the SimECNY AML dataset, highlighting multi-source knowledge fusion and cross-dimensional interaction. Table~\ref{tab:provenance} shows the complete provenance cards for both features.

\subsection{Case 1: Fan-In/Fan-Out Ratio (Network Topology)}

This feature fuses four knowledge assets spanning three types (rules, patterns, indicators) to produce a topology-aware asymmetry score. The key insight from RULE\_006 and RULE\_013 is that both scatter-in/gather-out layering and pyramid fund networks manifest as fan-in/fan-out asymmetry. PAT\_A03 further confirms that money mule wallets typically exhibit fan-in $\gg$ fan-out. IND\_15 adds a weighting factor based on abnormal transaction modes (many-to-many, one-to-many), amplifying the signal for wallets with suspicious mode distributions.

No syntactic method would produce this feature, as it requires understanding that directional imbalance combined with abnormal mode prevalence characterizes money mule behavior. The multiplicative weighting by $(1 + \text{abnormal})$ ensures that topological asymmetry alone produces a moderate score, but wallets with both asymmetry \emph{and} abnormal modes receive disproportionately higher values.

\subsection{Case 2: Cross-Dimensional Risk Interaction}

This feature demonstrates \emph{cross-dimensional interaction}: geographic context (account metadata) combined with temporal patterns (transaction logs), guided by expert knowledge. The critical insight from EXP\_05 is that neither geographic risk nor off-hour activity alone is sufficient evidence; only their conjunction indicates cross-border laundering. The multiplicative form encodes this domain constraint exactly: border-region accounts with normal trading hours score zero, as do off-hour traders in non-border regions.

Both cases illustrate capabilities that syntactic methods cannot replicate. The provenance cards in Table~\ref{tab:provenance} trace every design decision to specific knowledge assets, providing the audit trail required for regulatory review.
