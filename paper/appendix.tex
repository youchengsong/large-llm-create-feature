\section{Domain Knowledge Examples for the AML Dataset}
\label{sec:appendix_knowledge}

This appendix illustrates the five knowledge types provided to KnowFeat for the SimECNY anti-money laundering dataset ($\sim$200K digital yuan transactions). One representative example per type is shown; similar structured knowledge is compiled for all types (38 indicators, 15 rules, 12 expert opinions, 309 court documents).

\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{(i) Schema Metadata.}
Column names, data types, and basic statistics extracted from the transaction data. Example fields: \texttt{amount} (numeric), \texttt{wallet\_type} (categorical, 4 types: anonymous/personal/enterprise/merchant), \texttt{hour} (numeric, 0--23), \texttt{src}/\texttt{dst} (wallet identifiers), \texttt{transaction\_mode} (8 categories).

\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{(ii) Risk Indicator} (from 38-indicator risk taxonomy).
\begin{quote}
\small
\textbf{[IND\_05]} Dimension: transaction behavior anomaly. Risk class: amount \& frequency anomaly. Field: single-day transaction count. Direction: accounts whose daily transaction count exceeds regulatory thresholds indicate potential money mule activity for rapid fund channeling.
\end{quote}

\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{(iii) Detection Rule} (from 15-rule detection rule set).
\begin{quote}
\small
\textbf{[RULE\_006]} \emph{Scatter-in, gather-out.} Category: structuring. Logic: an account's daily incoming transaction count $\geq$ 20 AND daily total amount $\geq$ 1{,}000{,}000, with high incoming but low outgoing transaction count. Python hint: group by \texttt{(src, date)}, aggregate count and sum, filter accounts with many small inflows and few large outflows.
\end{quote}

\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{(iv) Expert Opinion} (from domain expert interviews).
\begin{quote}
\small
\textbf{[EXP]} Online ``running-score'' laundering pattern: anonymous wallet $\to$ real-name wallet $\to$ bank account chain transfers, typically operating after midnight. Transaction amounts range 300--5{,}000 yuan, sustained over 10--30 days, with daily volume reaching wallet limits. Key signals: concentrated nighttime transfers, anonymous-to-real-name chains, daily volume at capacity.
\end{quote}

\smallskip
\noindent\textbf{(v) Court Document Evidence} (from 309 court judgments).
\begin{quote}
\small
\textbf{[CASE]} Source: criminal judgment on concealing proceeds of crime. Pattern: the defendant used digital yuan wallets across 6 banks (ABC, WeBank, BoCom, PSBC, CIB, CMB) to create a multi-layer fund channeling network. Observable signals: funds transferred via digital yuan APP, routed through multiple bank wallets as intermediaries, corporate-to-personal account conversion before transfer out.
\end{quote}
