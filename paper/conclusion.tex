\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented KnowFeat, a knowledge-guided feature engineering framework that combines hierarchical domain knowledge injection, multi-stage verification (L1--L3), and intelligent search strategies within an LLM agent. Our experiments on seven public datasets demonstrate that KnowFeat achieves the best AUC on 4 out of 7 datasets and the best average rank (1.4) among all methods, while generating 3--10$\times$ fewer features than FeatLLM. The ablation study confirms that domain knowledge is essential for generating effective features, with the full knowledge hierarchy outperforming schema-only on 5 of 6 applicable datasets. Every generated feature is traceable to specific knowledge assets, providing the auditability required in regulated domains.

\textbf{Limitations and Future Work.}
Several directions remain for future investigation.
First, our current knowledge hierarchy is manually organized; automating knowledge extraction and structuring from heterogeneous sources (regulations, case law, expert interviews) is a promising direction.
Second, KnowFeat currently uses a single LLM for all reasoning; a multi-agent architecture with specialized agents for knowledge retrieval, feature proposal, and verification could further improve efficiency and quality.
Third, extending KnowFeat to high-stakes domains such as anti-money laundering, cybersecurity, and insurance fraud detection would validate the generality of the knowledge-guided approach under severe class imbalance and domain-specific constraints.
Finally, investigating how the L3 verification threshold affects the trade-off between feature quantity and quality is an important direction for improving KnowFeat's adaptiveness.
