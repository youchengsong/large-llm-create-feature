\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented KnowFeat, a knowledge-guided feature engineering framework that combines hierarchical domain knowledge injection, multi-stage verification (L1--L3), and auditable provenance within an LLM agent. On seven public datasets, KnowFeat achieves the best AUC on 4 of 7 and the best average rank (1.4) among seven methods, while requiring significantly fewer features than alternative LLM-based methods. On a real-world AML dataset with 200K transactions, 18 knowledge-guided features improve Recall by +2.1pp and F1 by +1.5pp over a strong 93-feature baseline---gains that are robust across different class balancing settings. The ablation study confirms that domain knowledge is essential, and every generated feature is traceable to specific knowledge assets.

\textbf{Limitations and Future Work.}
Our current knowledge hierarchy is manually organized; automating knowledge extraction from heterogeneous sources is a promising direction. Extending KnowFeat to additional high-stakes domains (e.g., cybersecurity, insurance fraud) would further validate the generality of the approach.
